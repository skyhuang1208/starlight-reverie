✦ HOW TO PLAY · 操作說明 ✦

Welcome to Starlight Reverie

Choose whichever control style feels most comfortable—both work at the same time!

🖱️ Style A · WASD + Mouse

  • A / D — Move Left / Right
  • W / Space — Jump & Double Jump
  • S / Shift — Slide / Drop Bar
  • Left Click — Normal Attack (No CD)
  • Right Click — Skill (CD)
  • Q — Ultimate Skill (15s CD)

⌨️ Style B · Arrows + 1 / 2 / 3

  • ← / → — Move Left / Right
  • ↑ / Space — Jump & Double Jump
  • ↓ / Shift — Slide / Drop Bar
  • 1 — Normal Attack (No CD)
  • 2 — Skill (CD)
  • 3 — Ultimate Skill (15s CD)
⚡ Dodge tracking thunderclouds (1s charge)! 🔫 Pip: Hold W / ↑ to shoot Attack, Skill & Ultimate straight UP! ⚔️ Lyra: Double-Jump + Attack for 30° Air-Slice!
